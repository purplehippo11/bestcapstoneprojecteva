Beyond Scared Strayed

The air smells of musk. Your sensory organs have been hijacked by the elixir of nostalgia. The image of a grande sized pumpkin spice latte from Starbucks gets implanted into your head. Yeah... it is definitely fall. You came here for one thing and one thing only. Your beloved cat "cookie butter". Her routine checkup at the animal hospital begins at 9:00 PM. You must rescue her before time runs out... Or else the evil animal veterinarian will cast a spell on cookie butter to bake her into a double chocolate chunk cookie. The time is 8:14 PM. Under the crimson sky, the wind brushes your shoulder with the ever so slightest sincerity. Cling HARD onto this fleeting feeling, as it will fade away soon. SCREEEECH!!! What the heck was that??? An entire colony of vampire bats emerge from the backdrop of fog. The swarm converges toward you as you sprint away as fast as you can! There is only one place for you to retreat to: Danny's haunted mansion.
